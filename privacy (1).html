Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What information is collected

Plainwork Products are built to work with as little personal data as possible. To generate content, you provide business information (like a business name or a customer review) directly into the tool. This content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response, and is not stored on Plainwork's servers beyond what's needed to complete that request.

2. Access codes

Your access code and unlock status are stored locally in your browser (via localStorage), not on a central server tied to your identity. Clearing your browser data will require re-entering your code.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider (Lava.top). Plainwork does not directly collect or store your card details -- that information is handled entirely by the payment processor under its own privacy and security practices.

4. What is not collected

Plainwork Products do not use tracking cookies, third-party ad trackers, or behavioral analytics to profile visitors.

5. Cookies

The Products may use strictly technical, first-party browser storage (such as localStorage) to remember your access code and language preference on your own device. This is not a tracking cookie and is not shared with any third party.

6. How long data is kept

Content you submit for generation (such as a business name or a customer review) is processed to generate a response and is not retained afterward. Payment records are kept by the payment processor (Lava.top) according to their own retention policy, which Plainwork does not control.

7. Your rights

You can ask what data, if any, is associated with you, and request it be deleted, by writing to kssw117@gmail.com. Since Products are designed to avoid storing personal data centrally, most requests can typically be resolved by simply clearing your own browser's local storage.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to know what data (if any) is associated with you, can be sent to kssw117@gmail.com.
